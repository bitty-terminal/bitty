Bitty Han regression fixture
============================

`crates/bitty-render/assets/bitty-han-test.ttf` is a subset of Noto Sans CJK
SC Regular 2.004 (the `SC` member of `/usr/share/fonts/noto-cjk/NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc`,
the Linux face named by `bitty_config::types::CJK_FALLBACK_FAMILY`), renamed
to "Bitty Han Test" (SIL OFL 1.1, section 3). It carries U+0020-U+007E plus
the twelve scalars of the reported line `请问饿七五二二头痛医头用` and nothing
else.

The face exists only for `crates/bitty-render/tests/shaped_mixed_script_fallback.rs`
(bitty#3021): it is compiled in by `src/embedded.rs` as `HAN_TEST_BYTES`, is
not a member of any font fallback chain, and no production path loads it, so
the Han half of the mixed-script regression resolves on a host that ships no
CJK face instead of skipping.

The subset keeps only the scalars listed above; hinting, layout tables
(GSUB/GPOS/GDEF), and glyph names are dropped, and CFF outlines are
desubroutinized. Regeneration is documented in
`crates/bitty-render/assets/generate-han-test-font.sh` and
`crates/bitty-render/assets/README.md`.
